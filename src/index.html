Get your free routing key

Routes come from openrouteservice, a free routing service run by HeiGIT, a non-profit. Each person uses their own key, so it takes about two minutes once.

  1. Go to account.heigit.org and create a free account (the free Standard plan).
  2. Find your openrouteservice API key in your account and tap its copy button.
  3. Come back here, open Setup: routing key, paste the key and tap Plan my run.

The key stays in this browser only. The free plan covers 2,000 route requests a day (a plan uses up to 12) and is for personal, non-commercial use.

"routing failed (403)": the key was refused. Copy it again, or create a new key in your account.
"Couldn't reach the routing service": check the key, then try again in a minute.
